Privacy policy

Household assistant is a private tool run by one family for its own members. Only those members can authorize it.

What it accesses

With each member's consent, read-only access to their Gmail and, where they choose, files in their Google Drive.

How the data is used

  • Messages and files are read only to organise the family's own bills, statements, insurance and school notices.
  • Data is stored on the family's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The tool never sends, deletes or modifies email.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Any member can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Use the support email shown on Google's consent screen for this app.
